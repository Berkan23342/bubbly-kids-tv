Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Bubbly Kids TV ("we") handles information for our YouTube channel and for "Kids Channel Uploader", the internal tool we use to publish our videos.

Our videos and viewers

Bubbly Kids TV videos are published on YouTube and are marked as "made for kids". We do not collect, store or ask for any personal information from viewers, including children. Viewing happens on YouTube and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube's own policies for children's content. We do not run our own comments, sign-ups, contests or tracking.

Kids Channel Uploader and Google user data

Kids Channel Uploader uses the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the owner of the Bubbly Kids TV channel, on the owner's own computer, to upload videos the owner created to the owner's own channel.

  • What it accesses: only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on the signed-in channel.
  • What it stores: an OAuth access/refresh token, kept only on the owner's computer, so scheduled uploads can run. No other Google account data is read or stored.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or for training AI models.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the token file removes all stored data.

Kids Channel Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service.

Children's privacy

Our content is designed for young children, so we never ask children for personal information and have no way for them to send it to us. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our YouTube channel page: youtube.com/@BubblyKidsTVchannel.
